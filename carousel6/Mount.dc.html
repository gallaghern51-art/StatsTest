PLATE 05 — MOUNT & ATTACHMENT
How it holds on
REPLACES THE STOCK FILLING CHAMBER
NO TOOLS · FULLY REVERSIBLE

The carousel takes the filling chamber's place in the chamber housing — drop in, twist to the click, and the whole assembly is captive. Three lugs pull a graphite face gasket down onto the heater's outlet ring, so the same twist that retains the unit is what seals it. A thin-wall neck above the seat keeps that heat from climbing straight into the disc.

What the mount has to do
01
Seal. Every gram of airflow has to go through the pod, not around the collar. The lugs supply the clamping load; the gasket does the rest.
02
Carry the load. Roughly 380 g cantilevered 78 mm above the deck, plus whatever a full balloon pulls sideways.
03
Break the heat path. Thin wall, short contact, and no direct metal run from the outlet ring to the disc.
04
Come off cleanly. Reverse twist, lift, refit the stock chamber. Nothing on the Volcano is altered.
THE ONE HARD DEPENDENCY
Everything else here can be drawn from first principles. This interface cannot — it has to be measured off a Volcano Hybrid before a single part is cut.
