FOUR-STATION ROTARY POD CHANGER
HOST DEVICE — VOLCANO HYBRID
REV A · CONCEPT ONLY

A pod magazine that replaces the Volcano's filling chamber. Four sealed pods are loaded away from the machine, dropped into a rotating disc, and indexed one at a time over the heater outlet by a small geared motor. Each pod carries its own dose, so a session is set up once and stepped through by hand — no reloading a hot chamber between bags, and no guessing at grind weight while the unit is at temperature.
