NEWEST FIRST
Everything here is simulated from the concept's own numbers — heat-up follows the Volcano Hybrid's published 40-second figure to 180 °C, bag-fill time scales with bed depth, and a pod yields one to three bags depending on its riser. Nothing has been built or measured. The interlock shown — the drive refusing to move while the plenum sensor reports flow — is the behaviour the controller is meant to enforce, not tested behaviour.
