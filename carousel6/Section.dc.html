PLATE 02 — SECTION A–A
Air path & sealing
CUT ON THE PLANE THROUGH THE AXIS
AND THE ACTIVE STATION

Nothing that seals ever slides. The disc runs at a fixed height with clearance above and below; the two sealing faces retract to let it turn and close again on the detent. Hot air crosses to the active station before it reaches the flower, so the only surfaces vapour touches are the pod, the clamp, and the stub — all of which lift out.

Detail X — the hot seal
≤ 230 °C

No elastomer anywhere near the heat. A lapped stainless face closed onto graphite foil by spring preload, retracted by the same cam that frees the disc — so the face is only ever loaded when stationary, and never wiped.

Detail Y — the cool seal
vapour side

The outlet side runs far cooler than the inlet, so a silicone O-ring is fine here. The clamp lifts 2.5 mm — enough to clear the pod flange and the counterbore lip — and the whole plenum pulls off the stub for cleaning.
