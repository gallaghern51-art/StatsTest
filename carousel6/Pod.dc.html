PLATE 04 — THE POD
One pod, four servings
BORE MATCHES THE STOCK SCREEN Ø30
DOSE SET BY A DROP-IN RISER

The pod bore is the same diameter as the Volcano's own screen, so a pod at full depth holds a complete stock bowl and presents the same bed geometry to the pump. Smaller servings come from a perforated riser that lifts the floor — the pod's outside never changes, so every station seals at the same two planes whatever dose is in it.

■ Three pads carry the pod — the only conduction path into the disc.
Screens are captive top and bottom, so a loaded pod travels either way up.

Bed depth is compared against a stock bowl at the same Ø30 screen diameter; at equal area, restriction tracks depth.
Or skip custom pods entirely

Storz & Bickel already sell a filling chamber with a reducer that seats one of their dosing capsules in the Volcano — so the reduction geometry is proven hardware, not a guess. The same idea drops into this pod as a sleeve: the carousel then runs on stock capsules, and a pod becomes a carrier rather than a consumable.

The trade is dose range. A stock capsule is around 0.15–0.20 g, so this route covers the small end only — worth it as the first prototype, since it removes the pod from the build entirely and leaves just the disc and the two seals to prove. The sleeve bore has to be measured off a real capsule; the published figures disagree and the manufacturer's own pages would not load here.
